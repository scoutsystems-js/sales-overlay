How it works

Set it up in 60 seconds.
Coach you in real time.

Scout runs quietly in the background. Open it, click Start, and it takes over — no setup between calls.

1

Download & Launch

Install the Mac app. Scout appears as a dark bar at the top of your screen — always visible, never in the way.

2

Start Your Call

Click Start in Scout. It begins listening to both you and your prospect using your mic and system audio — no special setup.

3

Follow the Prompts

Scout reads the conversation and displays your next line in the overlay. Deliver it, let the prospect respond, and your next prompt appears.

4

Handle Every Objection

When Scout detects an objection, it instantly switches to the right framework and gives you the exact response to say.

Features

Built for closers.
Not sales managers.

Everything in Scout is designed around one moment — you on a live call, needing to know what to say right now.

🎯

Live AI Teleprompter

Scout follows the conversation in real time and always shows you the ideal next statement and question — matched to where you are in the call.

🛡️

Instant Objection Handling

Money, spouse, think about it, no time — Scout detects objections the moment your prospect speaks and immediately shows you the proven framework response.

🗺️

7-Stage Call Framework

Scout tracks where you are across Introduction, Set, Discovery, Transition, Pitch, Close, and Objection Handling — and coaches appropriately for each stage.

✅

Discovery Tracker

A live checklist that auto-fills as the call progresses. Always know if you've confirmed budget, decision maker, pain, timeline, and more before you pitch.

🔇

Zero Interference

Scout never pops up while you're speaking. The overlay stays silent when you're talking and only updates after your prospect responds.

🔌

Works on Any Platform

Zoom, Teams, FaceTime, phone calls — if your prospect can hear you, Scout can hear them. No integrations or plugins required.
