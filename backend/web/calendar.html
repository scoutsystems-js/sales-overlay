Your calendar stays private

Scout reads your primary Google Calendar for the dates shown and shows only appointments with the verified Sober Living Riches GHL booking details. Event details are visible only to you. Your manager and Scout admins can see how many appointments you have, not event details.

Scout will not create or change events. It captures qualifying appointments from 90 days before today through 90 days after today in your primary calendar time zone and keeps only a minimal appointment history: occurrence identity, schedule and time zone, a derived Zoom meeting ID when available, the calendar title as an unverified label when it contains no link or credential-like value, and one unambiguous external attendee email. It does not save descriptions, meeting links, attendee names or arrays, or raw event payloads, and this inspection is not sent to AI.
