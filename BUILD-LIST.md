### ⚠⚠⚠ SECOND QUESTION FOR JUSTIN — MOVING A REP MOVES THEIR HISTORY

**`fathom_calls` carries only `user_id`, and team scope resolves `managed_by` at query time.** So reassigning a rep **retroactively moves every past quarter's calls, cash, close rate and objections** from one company's totals to the other. **Reported and NOT changed** — the fix is either freezing history (snapshot the team on each call) or accepting the retroactive move, and that is a **reporting decision**. ⚠ It also compounds with the naming caveat: a client with two sales managers is already two companies, so "move a rep between managers" and "move a rep between companies" are the same action today.
