# Scout site design: Stark HUD

Status: approved new site design, recorded 2026-09-12. This is a visual design specification and implementation handoff. It is not deployed. The byte-exact approved source is saved at [docs/design/scout-coaching-stark-approved.html](docs/design/scout-coaching-stark-approved.html), with an openable standalone preview at [docs/design/scout-coaching-stark-preview.html](docs/design/scout-coaching-stark-preview.html). Both are saved inside the iCloud sales-overlay folder alongside this guide.

## Approval and scope

Justin's final approval: “save every detail of this this is the new site design.” The selected concept followed his instruction to return to blocks, review and improve the work, and make it look “like it was built by tony stark/ ironman.” Preserve the engineered cockpit feel through opaque floating panels, controlled edge lighting, precise corner traces, a luminous instrument ring, and slow white/gray HUD motion over the forest-green background. This is Scout's visual identity; do not add Iron Man logos, character art, fake system readouts, or unrelated controls.

Justin approved the Stark/block mockup as the new Scout site design on 2026-09-12. The request that led to the final selection treatment was: “make the name and menu selected look like this, reduce background gradient visibility by 5%.” The attached screenshot is the visual reference for the selected name and menu state: a dark forest green interior, brighter at the left, a dimmer right side, a muted pale outline, and a thin luminous green left edge.

The visual system is site-wide. Only Team Coaching is mocked today. The page is a static concept showing the site chrome, navigation, team coaching content, states, and responsive geometry. The earlier seamless treatment was rejected; preserve the Stark HUD/block treatment as the approved direction. This approval changes visual properties expressly covered here. It does not repeal existing product, metric, data, access, or content rules. In particular, the approved UI accent is `#09d543` where this design explicitly uses it; the archived wordmark asset keeps its original `#09E046` fill.

Amber remains reserved for the ring and the two amber score bars; do not recolor unrelated text or invent targets.

## Layout and responsive behavior

Desktop page grid: columns `190px minmax(0,1fr)`, rows `51px auto auto auto auto auto`, column gap 16px, padding `0 18px 26px`, minimum height 0. Header spans both columns. Nav occupies column 1, rows 2–4, with a 17px top margin. Main content is `display:contents`; title, filters, focus, workspace, and strengths occupy the declared grid rows. Main content max width is 1180px, with the source's 42px horizontal content padding where the grid layout is not active.

At max-width 700px, the page becomes block layout with no page padding; the top remains full width. Nav becomes a two-column grid with 12px margin and 2px gap; footer spans both columns. Main becomes block. Title and filters stack vertically; workspace becomes one column; members becomes a two-column grid with header/details spanning both columns; score cards become two columns; strengths become one column; detail header stacks; evidence rows become blocks. Panel corner radius becomes 12px. The gauge is 82×82px. At max-width 380px, score cards become one column. Preserve this geometry and the full-width blocks when implementing.

The approved browser QA measured the root at 992px wide in a 1024px viewport with no horizontal overflow, and 358px wide in a 390px viewport with no horizontal overflow. Computed final panel fills were `#101811` for focus and `#0c0f0d` for members/detail; those panels had no residual `background-image`. The HUD orbit reported the `scout-stark-hud-orbit` animation. Treat those measurements as archive verification, not as permission to alter production layout rules.

## HUD motion

The decorative HUD uses only three animation paths. The orbit group rotates 360 degrees around `600px 420px` over 82s, linear, infinitely. The scan line translates from `-10px` to `880px` over 28s, linear, infinitely. HUD marker circles move from `(0,0)` to `(13px,-9px)` over 67s, ease-in-out, infinitely, alternate; marker 2 uses 91s with -19s delay and marker 3 uses 74s with -37s delay. The HUD is masked, low contrast, and behind content. Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, orbit, markers, and scan all use `animation:none!important`.

## Interaction contract

The mockup is static. Nav links, member buttons, focus plus signs, date/company controls, the toggle, and “View exchange →” are visual examples unless the implementing product wires them to existing routes and data. The native `details` summary is functional in the mockup and expands/collapses its no-calls text. The selected nav item and selected member are visual states only in the artifact. The date control is a white control in the mockup and must retain its visual treatment when made functional. Do not make the mocked counts, scores, or text into fake live metrics; connect them only to approved existing backend computations and access rules.

## Implementation checklist

- Use the archived Stark HTML and final cascade as the visual reference; consolidate exploratory overrides only when resolved styles and the rendered appearance still match the approved reference. Preserve the archived file itself byte-for-byte.
- Preserve existing backend, database, metric definitions, permissions, manager/team membership, active-user filtering, real-call exclusions, and accessibility behavior.
- Keep semantic elements and accessible labels from the mockup; ensure selected navigation and member states expose the correct state to assistive technology when wired.
- Keep panels opaque and layered above the decorative HUD. Keep the HUD `aria-hidden`, noninteractive, masked, and motion-reduced for users who request reduced motion.
- Keep the `#09d543` accent and `#fbbf24` amber ring treatment exactly where approved. Do not carry the new visual properties into unrelated product rules or replace production data with the mocked numbers.
- Verify desktop, narrow, and reduced-motion renders against the archived preview before shipping. This design is not live until a separate implementation and deployment has been explicitly completed.

## Implementation boundaries and review improvements

The approval preserves a visual direction, not every shortcut in the static artifact. The mockup's header is in normal flow; the production sticky-header behavior, existing background preference setter, accessible mesh toggle, links, filters, and functional member selection must be retained when this appearance is implemented. The artifact contains inherited CSS overrides, unused selectors (including `.stage` and selectors looking for the former accent), and browser-tolerated wrapper nesting. Rebuild clean product markup and shared design tokens rather than pasting the fragment over the live dashboard. Preserve metric bands: 69 is amber in this snapshot; dynamic scores must continue to use Scout's existing band logic, rather than being permanently amber. Only this Team Coaching page has been visually approved in detail; apply its materials and styling consistently to other pages without inventing page layouts or deleting their features.

The final review enlarged the ring to 98px (82px on narrow screens), separated `/100` below the number, added its fine perimeter ticks, raised supporting text to 11px, gave each strength action a 96px minimum lane with no wrapping, strengthened the Coaching Focus edge, and softened the supporting panel edges. These are part of the approved final state. The `RECENT CALLS IN THIS PERIOD` footer label remains in the coaching detail panel. The three good-band stage bars are green (`#09d543`) at 77%, 73%, and 80%; the two mid-band bars are amber (`#fbbf24`) at 69% and 61%. Their exact geometry, panel padding, and all remaining style details are retained in the appendix and full archive below.
